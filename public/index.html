HOW IT WORKS

Talk. Get told the truth. Go again.

  1. Tell us who you are. A Class 10 student and a supply-chain manager should not get the same questions. Add your resume if you have one.
  2. Speak your answers. Tap once and talk. Your interviewer replies out loud, in real time.
  3. Read the report. Scores broken down, the phrases to drop, the words to use instead — saved so you can watch yourself improve.
